COASTAL EXPLORER — VERSION 18 / STREET LIFE

PLAY ONLINE

DOWNLOAD / BUNDLED MAP
Extract this ZIP into a new folder. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
Wait for the included real map to unpack, then select a vehicle and use
DRIVE / LAUNCH. Local play starts immediately without any server.
Run node server/relay.cjs to serve the game with its local relay, or use

CONTROLS
WASD / arrows: drive. S: brake / reverse. Space: handbrake.
C: cockpit / third person. G: showroom, purchases and upgrades. M: map.
R: recover. H: settings. Esc: pause. L: headlights. [ / ]: indicators.
V: hazards. F: one F18 cannon round; civilian vehicles are unarmed. Speaker button: sound.
LandCruiser: hold Shift for clutch; Q downshift / E upshift. Starts in 1st.
Helicopter: W/S ascend/descend; A/D yaw; arrows cyclic.
Fighter: W/S throttle; arrows pitch/roll; A/D yaw; Shift boost; Space cannon.
Boat: W/S throttle/brake/reverse; A/D steering; Space slows for docking.

MONEY AND SHOP
The original four vehicles are owned. Fresh careers start at $65,000.
Ordinary movement does not pay. Link road drifts, sustain road speed over
120 km/h or survive active five-star chases for skill rewards. Start
vehicle-specific jobs and checkpoint races from ACTIVITIES.
Teleporting or changing vehicles cancels the job. Cash, ownership and
mods save automatically in this browser. Hosted and downloaded copies
have separate saves. Click a showroom card or dropdown, then BUY or
DRIVE / LAUNCH. Paint costs $750; compatible upgrades have three levels.

MAP
Click to place a pin; TRAVEL TO PIN teleports there. Drag to pan; scroll
or + / - zooms. Search suburbs, streets or known mapped addresses.
Full address lookup is optional and online. Approximate location is
shown below the map; exact house numbers are not invented.

ADMIN
Open ADMIN, enter Iamsteve and click UNLOCK ADMIN. Your own player
controls work locally without a server: set/gift money, wanted levels,

A new relay uses the same code by default. Anyone knowing the code can

MAP AND SOUND ACCURACY
All 250 mapped places, 28,596 known building outlines and the original
measured elevations remain. Terrain uses the full 256-step grid.
Additional houses in unmapped residential blocks are estimated, not
surveyed footprints. They can be disabled in settings. Geographic
coverage is incomplete; not every real-world house is mapped.
A genuine licensed LandCruiser engine recording is NOT bundled. Sound
is synthesised by default: the 4.5 L turbo-diesel V8 has eight firing
pulses per cycle and gradual turbo spool. This is NOT a real recording.
Settings accepts an engine audio recording
you own, for RPM-linked playback on the selected vehicle in this tab.

DAMAGE AND COCKPIT GAUGES
Bullets damage the component they strike: engine, driver, radiator, oil
system, fuel tank or body. Engine failure removes powered acceleration.
Oil/coolant leaks can cause delayed seizure; critical fuel damage can
burn and explode. Direct driver hits can be fatal. Severe impacts dent
the actual model, throw debris and can tumble or ignite the wreck.
The cockpit monitor shows oil pressure/temperature, coolant temperature,
fluid levels and warnings. Watch it after taking damage.
A fatal run shows WASTED/WRECKED/DESTROYED and returns to the garage
after about eight seconds. Choose a vehicle to respawn. Recovery alone
does not repair components. Admin repair restores fluids and damage.
Fuel explosions and crash deformation are arcade approximations.
V8 wall hits directionally crush the mesh and keep a fatal wreck at the
wall instead of bouncing away. See docs/V8-RELEASE.md and verification.

V10 AIRSPACE, COLLISIONS AND ATC
Unauthorized flight raises stars faster. Police air support and airport
radar make helicopter escape harder. Fighters fire only through a narrow
nose cone, along their actual forward axis, with line-of-sight checks.
ATC REQUEST LANDING uses a selected map pin or the ground below you.
Acknowledgement -> site/weather/access/traffic review -> clear, hold or
refuse. Clearances last 120 seconds within 220 m of the approved site.
Water, unapproved roofs, private grounds, severe weather and active
high-priority pursuit can cause refusal. These are game rules, not real
aviation clearances or a property-boundary survey.
Scenery collisions include buildings, generated trees, poles, vehicles,
players, terrain/shore contact and aircraft roof strikes. Fast movement
uses swept contact checks. Critical damage raises visible warning cards
with a vehicle/component marker. This remains arcade crash deformation.
See docs/V10-RELEASE.md and docs/V10-VERIFICATION.md.

V11 MODELS, WORKSHOPS AND INTERFACE
Y hides/shows driving information while keeping maps. Hold left mouse to
look around the cab or orbit in third person; release to recenter.
M opens the live map without pausing. Click then SET PRIVATE WAYPOINT;
only you see it. Existing click-to-teleport remains available.

Gold + and H markers show 32 OSM mechanic locations. Drive into the
marked bay and stop, or land gently on one of nine suitable rooftop H
pads with gear down. Click REPAIR and remain stopped for 5-6 seconds.
The displayed fee is charged once on completion. Moving cancels service.
Entrances/pads are game additions, not claims of real-world helipads.
Search a workshop name or workshop/repair/mechanic to find locations.

Use the small ATC corner button or T to request landing at your map pin.
An approved site is marked in the scene and both maps. Landing without
ATC or mission clearance immediately raises wanted levels. You cannot
enter the vehicle garage while airborne. Entering it after landing
clears damage and fire from that run. Physical workshops preserve jobs.

Police patrols redistribute around your district and higher stars bring
more dedicated units, including dispatch from mapped police stations.
Civilian vehicles keep their identity. Rescue dispatch grants airport
and low-urban-flight clearance; military exclusions and deliberate
weapon offences still apply. Medical pickup scenes show a crashed car;
hoist rescues show a capsized boat on mapped water.

Models are original simplified geometry. All 18 previews are actual
renders of the same meshes used in play and by network players.
See docs/V11-RELEASE.md and docs/V11-VERIFICATION.md.

V12 AIR RESCUE + COMBAT
F18: hold Space for nose-mounted cannon fire (578 rounds, heat lockout).
The sight follows the nose, including when you look around. X ejects
a living pilot. An automatic chute opens; WASD steers; land before G
returns to the garage. The abandoned plane falls independently.
Z deploys two tracking-disruption flares, with an eight-second recharge.

for A$4,000 once per successful run. Firing at scenery/traffic is still

Temperatures rise gradually with load/RPM and cool with reduced load
and airflow. F18 oil/exhaust heat increases with afterburner and latched
throttle even after W is released. Its gauge reads EXHAUST TEMP rather
than claiming a liquid coolant tank. All temperatures are game models.

Workshop H pads accept a safe landing across the usable area, rather
than demanding an exact centre point. Missing/stale touchdown records
recover after 1.5 seconds of stable contact. Hovering or gear-up arrivals
do not count. The prompt explains what is missing. An engine failure
with full-looking bars is serviceable. Stop, click REPAIR and wait six
seconds; departure cancels the timer without a charge. The old workshop
prompt no longer stays over the vehicle garage.

V13 STREET PURSUIT
Roads are wider by default, with their real OSM centre lines retained.
Settings > Road width offers source widths, Wider streets (default)
and Extra wide. Footpaths retain their own widths. These are game road
widths; the original map database is unchanged.
Ground uses finer grass/earth/gravel detail and mapped land-use colours
for sand, woodland, farmland, residential and industrial areas.

The minimap shows the road limit and a red speeding warning. Sustained
speeding more than 5 km/h over the limit, seen by a police patrol for
1.25 seconds, raises wanted levels. Reports have a three-second interval.
Limits use mapped speed tags, with road-class defaults where absent.
These are game rules, not a legal guide to real posted speed limits.

Stop near pursuing police and an officer approaches your driver's door.
Remain stopped for the arrest countdown: BUSTED, one fine and an
eight-second return to the garage. U signals surrender and shortens
the countdown. Driving away or breaking detection interrupts it.
No wanted level means no arrest. The game fine is A$250 + A$250 per
star, capped at the cash you have. Owned vehicles/upgrades remain.

Cars, bikes, boats and all civilian/rescue helicopters have no guns.
Select an owned F18 in the garage's Weapons bay to buy three levels of
ammunition (+25% per level), cannon cooling (+30% per level) or defensive
flare capacity (+4 per level). First levels cost A$6,000/A$9,000/A$4,000;
higher levels cost proportionally more. Weapon levels save in the same
career as vehicle mods. Launching applies your purchased loadout.

All earlier F18 cannon, steerable ejection, gunnery challenge, thermal
response and raised repair-deck fixes are included.
See docs/V13-RELEASE.md and docs/V13-VERIFICATION.md.
