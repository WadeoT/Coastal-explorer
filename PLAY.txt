OFFLINE
Extract this ZIP into a new folder. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
Wait for the included real map to unpack, then select a vehicle and use
DRIVE / LAUNCH. If local HTML is blocked, use the included Mac/Windows
launchers with Python 3, or run python3 serve.py.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrows: drive. S: brake / reverse. Space: handbrake.
C: cockpit / third person. G: showroom, purchases and upgrades. M: map.
R: recover. H: settings. Esc: pause. L: headlights. [ / ]: indicators.
V: hazards. F: arcade counterattack. Speaker button: sound.
LandCruiser: hold Shift for clutch; Q downshift / E upshift. Starts in 1st.
Helicopter: W/S ascend/descend; A/D yaw; arrows cyclic.
Fighter: W/S throttle; arrows pitch/roll; A/D yaw; Shift boost; Space cannon.
Boat: W/S throttle/brake/reverse; A/D steering; Space slows for docking.

MONEY AND SHOP
The original four vehicles are owned. Fresh careers start at $65,000.
Movement DOES NOT PAY. Start delivery jobs or checkpoint races from
ACTIVITIES. Reach the ordered targets to earn the displayed reward.
Teleporting or changing vehicles cancels the job. Cash, ownership and
mods save automatically in this browser. Hosted and downloaded copies
have separate saves. Click a showroom card or dropdown, then BUY or
DRIVE / LAUNCH. Paint costs $750; compatible upgrades have three levels.

MAP
Click to place a pin; TRAVEL TO PIN teleports there. Drag to pan; scroll
or + / - zooms. Search suburbs, streets or known mapped addresses.
Full address lookup is optional and online. Approximate location is
shown below the map; exact house numbers are not invented.

MULTIPLAYER
GitHub Pages hosts the game; a relay server is needed for reliable
internet room play. The ZIP includes server/relay.cjs and render.yaml.
See docs/V6-RELEASE.md for the Render deployment link and setup.
After deployment, enter its HTTPS URL in Multiplayer, host a room and
share the generated link. Guests join using that link. The relay-hosted
site fills the server URL automatically. Eight players per room.
Local server: node server/relay.cjs -> http://localhost:8080 .
Optional PeerJS/direct WebRTC can work without a relay on compatible
networks. If the browser cannot gather candidates, use the relay.

ADMIN
Use the ADMIN button; type help. Solo players and room hosts have admin.
Examples: give jet | cash 250000 | repair | wanted 0 | weather rain
          time 21 | tp -26.6145 153.0993 | noclip off | players

MAP AND SOUND ACCURACY
All 250 mapped places, 28,596 known building outlines and the original
measured elevations remain. Terrain uses the full 256-step grid.
Additional houses in unmapped residential blocks are estimated, not
surveyed footprints. They can be disabled in settings. Geographic
coverage is incomplete; not every real-world house is mapped.
A genuine licensed LandCruiser engine recording is NOT bundled. Sound
is synthesised by default: the 4.5 L turbo-diesel V8 has eight firing
pulses per cycle and gradual turbo spool. This is NOT a real recording.
Settings accepts an engine audio recording
you own, for RPM-linked playback on the selected vehicle in this tab.

V7 DAMAGE AND COCKPIT GAUGES
Bullets damage the component they strike: engine, driver, radiator, oil
system, fuel tank or body. Engine failure removes powered acceleration.
Oil/coolant leaks can cause delayed seizure; critical fuel damage can
burn and explode. Direct driver hits can be fatal. Severe impacts dent
the actual model, throw debris and can tumble or ignite the wreck.
The cockpit monitor shows oil pressure/temperature, coolant temperature,
fluid levels and warnings. Watch it after taking damage.
A fatal run shows WASTED/WRECKED/DESTROYED and returns to the garage
after about eight seconds. Choose a vehicle to respawn. Recovery alone
does not repair components. Admin repair restores fluids and damage.
Fuel explosions and crash deformation are arcade approximations.
See docs/V7-RELEASE.md and docs/V7-VERIFICATION.md.
