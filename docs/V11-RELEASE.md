# V11 — Coastal Motorsport

26 October 2026.

The garage, player exterior, remote players, rescue scenes and police aircraft now share one vehicle-model dispatcher. All 18 cards use actual renders of those same meshes. The AS350 has a rounded cabin, tapered tail, fixed skids, three main blades and two tail blades. The LifeFlight/RACQ-inspired variants have rescue cabins, twin turbine housings, five main blades, four tail blades, hoist equipment and retractable tricycle gear. Road vehicles have different body proportions, cab/bed/wagon layouts, windows, wheels and identifying details; the fighter has wings, twin fins/engines, tricycle gear and a cockpit stick. These remain original simplified models, not manufacturer CAD replicas.

## Repairs at mapped mechanics

32 car-repair workshops retain their actual OSM coordinates and building IDs. Nine sufficiently large roofs receive marked game helipads. Buildings have a drive-in opening, workshop signage and service props. Both maps show gold `+` workshop or `H` pad markers; name and mechanic/workshop searches locate them.

Drive into a bay and stop, or make a gentle touchdown centred on H with gear down. Click REPAIR and stay stopped for five seconds in a bay or six on a pad. The displayed component-based fee is charged once when service finishes; departure, takeoff, fire or a vehicle change cancels it. A wreck, dead driver or rough touchdown cannot use safe-landing repair. Physical service preserves a job and position. Menu garage entry clears old damage/fire for the lobby and ends the current job; airborne aircraft cannot enter it.

Workshop locations are real mapped businesses. Bay entrances, equipment, estimated heights and rooftop pads are game additions; they do not assert that real businesses have helipads. Coverage is limited to tagged buildings in the retained export.

## Driving, camera and maps

- Spawn/recovery clears slope targets; contact samples update suspension pitch/roll rather than retaining an old lean.
- Y toggles driving information, retaining maps. Physical dashboard instruments remain part of the model.
- Hold left mouse to look around the cab or orbit in third person. Release recentres smoothly. Aircraft switch clicks still work. Third person clears inherited cockpit bank to keep the horizon level.
- The full map stays live; opening it clears held driving keys. Other pause/settings dialogs still pause.
- Click the map then SET PRIVATE WAYPOINT. It appears in your maps/scene and is never included in multiplayer snapshots. CLEAR removes it.
- ATC uses a small corner button or T. Open it to request clearance at a map pin or below the aircraft. Approved sites appear in the scene and both maps until expiry. Illegal game airspace gives the map a light red tint.
- Physical unauthorized helicopter/fighter touchdowns raise wanted levels immediately, usually at least three stars, with higher penalties in airport/military zones. Initial spawning/recovery is not a touchdown. Mission landing approval also counts.

## Police and rescue work

Police identities remain separate from the 24 civilian road users. Wanted levels activate 2/4/6/9/12 dedicated police units. Existing roaming units join; reserves can dispatch from 12 retained mapped police station/beat buildings. Distant idle patrols redistribute to the current district; stuck pursuit units try to rejoin roads and can recycle outside view. Aircraft ground-interception targets spread across nearby roads instead of converging on one Spit point.

Medical transfers create a visible crashed car; hoist rescue creates a capsized boat on actual mapped water. Pickup/cancellation/completion removes the incident. Rescue dispatch grants its contract's airport clearance and low-urban-flight exemption with a 1,200 m ceiling. Mission pickup/destination landing sites are approved. Police aircraft do not fire on a peaceful active rescue. Military exclusion, flying outside mission permissions and deliberate weapon offences still count; this is not blanket immunity.

The real Sunshine Coast archive and saved career key remain unchanged. Play starts locally immediately; configured relays autojoin in the background. No public internet relay or Netlify site has been created in your account. A configured relay is required for friends to share a server.
