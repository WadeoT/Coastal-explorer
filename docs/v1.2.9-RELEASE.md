# v1.2.9 — Shared Driving

The next bundle after legacy V28 uses the requested dotted version format: **v1.2.9**, followed by **v1.3.0**. Previous downloads and URLs retain their original names. See [version history](VERSION-HISTORY.md) and the [remaining backlog](../TASKS.md).

## Driving with friends

On foot, approach within 4.5 m of a friend's passenger-capable car and press **E**. The prompt names the driver. Coupes have one passenger seat; other supported cars have three. **C** switches between the seat camera and an external chase view, and mouse drag looks around. **X** exits; leaving a moving vehicle inherits its velocity and can injure or kill your character on landing. Passenger position/pose follows the driver's pitch, roll and movement. A driver leaving, dying, changing out of a car or disconnecting releases passengers into normal on-foot play. Simultaneous seat claims resolve deterministically.

Passenger avatars appear seated to other players. The live roster identifies passengers. Riding does not let your avatar collide with its own driver or control their car. Your abandoned vehicle remains separate from the friend's vehicle; its damage is not transferred to theirs.

## Collisions and combat

One collision solver handles local contact with other players' cars and walking avatars. It separates overlapping bodies, applies mass-weighted impulses and sends bounded impact damage to the other player. Resting contact causes no damage; received duplicates do not repeat an impact. Existing collision settings still apply. Driving faster than relay snapshots arrive can still produce imperfect contact timing.

The F18's nose cannon now tests other players' actual body volumes against nearer world obstructions. A validated hit is applied by the recipient to their component zones: driver, engine, fuel, radiator, oil or body. Civilian cars and rescue helicopters remain unarmed. Cannon direction comes from the aircraft nose. Vehicle damage summaries cause visible front/rear/side deformation on remote cars, and repairs restore their geometry. Rotor-loss state also clears when repaired.

Community combat messages have bounded counts, age, coordinates, weapon type, direction, distance and event frequency. IDs suppress replay across redundant relays; public packets remain within the relay's 4 KB limit. Combat cannot invoke owner/admin actions. Stationary firing and passenger changes are included in signed-relay pose-change detection.

**Authority limit:** public relays forward peer messages; they do not run an authoritative game simulation. These checks improve consistency and reject malformed/impossible messages, but cannot make modified clients cheat-proof. Ordinary traffic and pursuit simulation remain local; friends' chase units are visual replicas. Packet loss, delay or third-party outages still affect multiplayer. Both friends should use v1.2.9 or newer.

## Easier dents and crumpling

Visible car-cage deformation now begins at a 4 m/s contact impact instead of 7 m/s. Outer beams yield at lower strain and retain stronger folds; front/rear crush resistance is reduced, with a smaller reduction for side structures. Cabin beam resistance, part-detachment thresholds and the four-active-cage/30 Hz rendering budget are retained. Impact coverage also reaches cage nodes above/below a mid-panel hit instead of missing low-energy side contacts. Local panel dents are deeper and affect a wider area, and smaller damage updates are visible on other players’ cars. This affects both player and NPC car cages.

## Fuel leaks, delayed fires and explosions

Crash-damaged tanks and fuel lines now leak more readily, including damaged lines after front/right-side impacts. Normal severe crashes first produce a gradual visible fuel leak and warning; an ignition-risk crash then catches fire after roughly 4–10 seconds. Retained heat, fuel remaining, impact severity and direction affect this delay. Once the fire begins, it burns for a further roughly 7–12 seconds before a fuel explosion. Existing fire and pending-ignition clocks cannot be reset by repeated impacts.

Instant explosions now require an exceptional combination of impact speed, extreme heat, heavy fuel load and a severely ruptured tank. Empty tanks and electric vehicles cannot produce petrol fires. Cool minor punctures can leak without igniting. Repair clears a pending fire. The same staged model runs on player and NPC vehicles, including abandoned wrecks. These are cinematic gameplay outcomes.

## Traffic recycling crash

The V28 report traced `Cannot read properties of undefined (reading material)` to `createTrafficCar` during `refreshTrafficVariety`. The lookup assumed every batched traffic model had a material named `bodywork`; the LandCruiser uses `79-sandstone-paint`. Traffic now locates an owned paint surface after batching, recognises the original LandCruiser material and has a bounded fallback for future shapes. The browser regression constructs every traffic-eligible civilian profile, including the LandCruiser and delivery truck, through the actual traffic builder.

## Other fixes and data collection
