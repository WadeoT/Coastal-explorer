# v1.3.4 — Living Coast

Rounded, shaded foliage replaces flat leaf diamonds. Mapped woods, heath, scrub and parks receive denser trees and low scrub; existing road, building, water and workshop exclusions remain. Scrub is part of the destructible vegetation collision system. Plants are procedural and guided by mapped land cover, not individually surveyed. Geometry is batched by material; extra scrub is capped at 80 clumps on Low and 280 otherwise.

Twenty drifting cloud groups change coverage, height and shading with weather and time of day. Rain/storm/fog also darkens the water. Nearby water has calm, moderate and rough conditions with different swell height, period and foam. The same bounded wave function moves boats; sheltered water receives reduced swell. Existing shoreline clipping and the 7.5 m inset remain, and the animated water grid does not increase in size.

Open H/settings → Ocean conditions to follow game weather or select a sea state. This preference persists. Vegetation density, clouds and animated water remain optional performance controls. Disabling visual waves does not disable boat swell physics. Conditions are simulated, not live weather or a marine forecast.

Verification: build/parse checks, analytic wave bounds and exposure tests, finite batched foliage buffers, and a Chromium real-map run exercising boat movement and rough-sea rendering. Earlier fleet/airfield requests remain tracked in TASKS.md.

The focused Chromium run passed with 20 cloud groups, saved rough-sea selection, an active shoreline-inset wave mesh, finite boat motion and no page/simulation errors. In this software-rendered local sample, median simulation time was 5.6 ms and render time 15.2 ms; these are diagnostic samples, not a hardware FPS guarantee.
