# v1.3.6 — Visible Impact

Crash skins use stronger beam displacement, deeper/wider permanent folds and impact shading stored on body vertices. Cars, bikes, boats, helicopters and fighters share the bounded visual cage system. NPC cars use this same treatment after actual player/NPC or NPC/world collision recording, rather than relying on smoke or a health change. Mesh geometry is isolated before modification so an untouched model is not deformed. Repair restores positions and original vertex colours.

Tagged mirrors detach at 10 m/s, other tagged body parts at 17 m/s and wheels at 22 m/s when close to the contact. Six recent creases per vehicle, four scheduled cage updates and 48 loose parts remain the bounds. This deliberately exaggerates visible damage; it is not a calibrated full soft-body solver. Component health and the existing leak-before-fire behaviour are unchanged.

Walking now resets inherited look state when leaving a vehicle, clears residual camera targeting/input transforms, and supports continuous mouse orbit without the previous ±180° stop. Hold left mouse and drag; C changes first/third person. First-person vertical aim uses the full look pitch. Camera position follows walking while retaining ground/obstacle clearance.

Road width estimates now account for shoulders on motorway/trunk roads and at least two opposing lanes on two-way segments. Explicit mapped widths take precedence; residential streets, tracks and walking paths keep their earlier rules. The bundled map contains 28 Sunshine Motorway approach segments in the tested Coolum latitude band, including three two-way segments tagged lanes=1. Those three now use an estimated 11 m source corridor (14.3 m with the default gameplay width scale). Centre lines and junction connections remain mapped; these widths are estimates, not a new road survey.

Focused Chromium checks cover an actual player/NPC head-on collision with visible deformation on both, separate front/rear/side NPC impacts, exact mesh/colour repair, untouched-model isolation, real Coolum approach tags/widths, walking follow, physical mouse drags through a full turn and first-person vertical aim. Fleet expansion remains tracked in TASKS.md.

The head-on passenger-car test produced about 1.02 m peak visible player-shell movement and 0.31 m on the NPC shell, with attached impact shading on both. Direct NPC front/rear/side checks produced 0.66/0.69/0.49 m peak coordinate displacement and restored to their original positions/colours. These are diagnostic examples, not calibrated crash predictions. All-class cage/hijack tests, seven-car scheduling, light-crumple/repair checks, beam bounds and 12 fuel-fire timing checks also passed without browser exceptions.
