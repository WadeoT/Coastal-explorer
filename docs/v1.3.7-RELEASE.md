# v1.3.7 — Smooth Roads & Local Detail

The reported Commodore exception came from reading suspensionBase after a wheel lost its metadata. Road-wheel animation now restores an absent metadata record, keeps a finite fallback radius and skips missing, detached or disposed wheels. Invalid wheel inputs cannot write NaN suspension transforms.

Short bridge ways used an invented sinusoidal arch for clearance. On the Sunshine Motorway south of Coolum, two affected bridges had peak synthetic grades of about 53% and 40%. Decks now interpolate the measured abutment elevations, giving approximately 1.46% and 2.09% grades on those ways; a third sampled bridge is 3.48%. The rendered road and wheel-contact queries use the same generated geometry. These are DEM-based estimates, not surveyed deck clearances. Mapped tree nodes that fall inside widened gameplay road corridors are excluded to avoid trunks blocking a lane.

Nearby low-rise irregular house footprints can now receive a pitched, distance-to-edge roof that preserves concave shapes such as L-shaped plans. Detail is limited to 700 m, at most 18 footprint vertices and 650 square metres per roof, with two refinement levels and shared batched materials. Flat roof tags and courtyards are preserved. Rectangular homes choose an entrance edge near a mapped road. Explicit height/storey tags remain authoritative; untagged house/detached/bungalow types use a 3.1 m eaves-height estimate rather than 4.5 m. Materials and colours continue to prefer available mapped tags/aerial roof samples. Unobserved facades and roof forms remain inferred, not exact Street View recreations.

Landscape land-use queries use 24 m cache cells rather than 96 m, retaining smaller mapped residential/woodland patches and bringing procedural vegetation and ground colours closer to their mapped boundaries. Real coastline, terrain, street centre lines and building footprints remain the source geometry; no new surveyed house data was added.

Verification: Chromium drives the two short motorway bridges southbound with a 100 km/h entry speed, checks actual rendered-ground support and no unintended damage/collision, then runs the Commodore wheel update with deliberately null metadata plus missing/disposed records. Additional checks cover linear bridge grades, concave roof boundaries, mapped height/storey precedence and small land-cover patch retention.

Major solo crashes (impact delta-v at least 14 m/s) can trigger a 2.2-second cinematic window: simulation slows to 22%, then eases back to normal during the last 0.6 seconds. A five-second cooldown prevents repeated impacts prolonging it indefinitely. H/settings → Slow-motion crashes toggles the saved preference. It applies only to explicit solo mode and cancels immediately on entering public play, connecting to multiplayer, pausing or opening the garage. Rendering keeps its normal cadence; no extra postprocessing or draw passes are added. Resetting/repairing a vehicle clears the effect.

Final real-map runs passed both southbound bridge crossings with health 100 and no recorded collision. Maximum contact-height increments were approximately 0.7 cm and 2.0 cm per physics step in those runs. Null wheel metadata on the Commodore and missing/disposed array entries produced finite transforms without exceptions. The real crash trigger and persisted slow-motion toggle passed; separate clock-controlled tests verify cooldown, normal-speed restoration and online exclusion. The prior NPC deformation, walking-camera and motorway-width regression also passed.
