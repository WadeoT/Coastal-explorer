# v1.3.9 — QTopo Terrain

The uploaded Queensland QTopo GeoPDF now supplies 38 printed spot-height controls for the physical landscape. Mount Coolum changes from approximately 197.65 m to 208 m and Mount Tinbeerwah from 218.73 m to 267 m at their registered symbols. The correction is smooth and local; terrain meshes, road draping, building placement and ground-height queries share it. Coast clipping and tile seams are preserved, with original low land/water and terrain outside the sheet unchanged.

Open **M → QTOPO** to overlay the actual topographic sheet. It works offline, stays geographically aligned through pan/zoom, retains live players, navigation and airspace markings, and caches its projected rendering. The minimap keeps its normal readable map. The original PDF is included with the download and available under `data/qtopo/source.pdf`.

This release calibrates spot heights; it does **not** reconstruct all contour lines. The existing measured DEM provides the surface between controls. The map's scale, coverage, datum limitations, attribution and rebuilding steps are documented in [the QTopo data notes](../data/qtopo/README.md). The standalone HTML is approximately 73 MB because the lossless topographic image is bundled. No additional terrain work runs in the simulation loop.

Verification covers all 38 control heights, finite terrain, identical shared tile borders, unchanged low-coast/outside-coverage nodes, matching shoreline corrections and the offline map toggle/cache. The southbound Sunshine Motorway bridge and null/disposed suspension-wheel regressions are rerun on the corrected terrain. All nine imported Life Model vehicles remain available from v1.3.8. Remaining work is tracked in TASKS.md.
