# v1.4.0 — Regional Aviation

Mapped regional airfields now have 17 runway centre lines across 10 real locations, including Caloundra and Noosa. Caloundra also uses its mapped apron/taxiways. Physical runway surfaces follow the terrain; airport labels appear on the regional map and minimap, and eligible airfields can be selected in the garage spawn dropdown. Private, closed and unknown-access strips remain marked as restricted.

The aircraft shop adds Cessna 172 Skyhawk, Piper PA-28 Cherokee and Beechcraft Baron G58 approximations, each unarmed with animated propeller(s), fixed Cessna/Piper gear, retractable Baron gear, cabin geometry, cockpit instruments and distinct dimensions/performance. W/S changes throttle, arrows pitch/roll, A/D controls rudder, and Space applies wheel brakes. These use piston flight handling, appropriate fuel-capacity estimates, low-speed stall warnings and no afterburner. X exits to ordinary on-foot movement with the existing injury/fall risk; these aircraft have no fighter ejection seat or parachute.

Two parked aircraft stream near eligible airfields. Walk beside one and press E to hijack it; the existing theft/wanted rules apply. They remain borrowed vehicles rather than free permanent shop purchases. Parked planes are solid and participate in the existing NPC vehicle damage system. Nearby streaming, stolen-state tracking and hourly world replacement are retained.

T requests clearance at a nearby suitable runway; approval checks pursuit, weather, access and conflicting players, and places an approach waypoint followed by the landing marker. The F/A-18 continues to request Sunshine Coast Airport. The tutorial and controls explain the new aircraft. Actual geometry supplies the shop previews.

[Source records and modelling limits](../data/airfields/README.md) distinguish mapped runways from estimated parking positions and procedural aircraft art. Verification covers source geometry, profiles/gear, takeoff, held throttle, ATC restrictions/corridors, unarmed behavior and on-foot hijack/exit. This bundle retains the uploaded Life Models and QTopo terrain/reference from v1.3.8–v1.3.9. Remaining combat/fleet work stays listed in [TASKS.md](../TASKS.md).

New downloadable bundles are published as GitHub Release assets to avoid filling the Pages site with additional large archives. Existing historical download URLs remain unchanged.

Aircraft geometry is batched while keeping propellers, wheels and detachable wing/tail nodes separate. The final preview render measured 25/25/26 meshes and roughly 2,100–2,240 vertices per aircraft, compared with 54–59 meshes before this batching. These are geometry measurements, not an FPS guarantee.
