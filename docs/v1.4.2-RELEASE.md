# v1.4.2 — Airport and Bushland

Sunshine Coast Airport's two parked civilian planes now use its **mapped GA STH apron near the southern hangars**, rather than a guessed point beside the middle of runway 13/31. The previous stream check used the airport's runway-centre coordinate; the GA apron is about 1.7 km away and could fall outside that cutoff. Streaming now measures distance to the actual parking position, validates the full footprint against the apron, roads, buildings, water and terrain, and retains the aircraft through scenery replacement. Aircraft remain borrowed when boarded, and stolen slots reset under the existing hourly rules.

Choose **SUNSHINE COAST · PARKED PLANES** in the garage spawn menu to arrive next to the aircraft. Press X to leave a car, walk beside a plane and press E. Blue aircraft arrows mark currently parked planes on both maps. Actual parking positions are gameplay placements inside the mapped apron, not observations of real aircraft. The normal airport/runway spawn remains available for flying your own aircraft.

The bundled OpenStreetMap additions include four Sunshine Coast aprons and 53 taxiway features, with source records and export timestamps retained. Together with Caloundra this gives six aprons and 58 taxiway features; all 17 runway lines across ten fields are retained. Mapped aprons/taxiways are rendered and protected from generated vegetation. Spatial surface indexing avoids reconstructing their polygons during every ground/vegetation query. See [source details](../data/airfields/README.md).

ATC now has a takeoff process. **T** or the request button selects TAKEOFF on the ground and LANDING in flight. Takeoff requests queue, enter review, check weather/access/pursuit and runway traffic, then grant a five-minute slot or hold/refuse the request. The panel supplies the runway and heading, with a ground runway waypoint before approval and an outbound waypoint after approval. Approval remains active while waiting on the ground and during departure; it expires or completes after leaving the local corridor. Helicopters can request a departure from an acceptable local site. Requests are simulation/gameplay rules, not real aviation procedures.

Regional airport approvals now resolve to the correct game-airspace zone IDs. Previously a runway identifier such as YBSU could be stored where enforcement expected `sunshine-airport`; a player could receive an approval while still being treated as unauthorised. Approved departure covers the airport taxi/departure area instead of only the runway's narrow landing corridor. Other altitude, restricted-zone and pursuit rules still apply.

Mapped forest/wood cover now produces denser nearby crowns with layered undergrowth. Scrub and heath favour low thickets with fewer trees, mangrove cover uses shorter crowns, and ordinary wetland/grassland/developed land does not acquire forest canopy. Exact mapped-polygon checks preserve small clearings and holes. Nearby plants receive detail first; outer crowns use simpler geometry. Canopy/undergrowth are combined into existing scenery batches, capped at 1,800/640 on normal settings and 700/120 on Low, and respect the saved vegetation slider. Roads, buildings, water, runways, aprons and taxiways are excluded. Exact plants, species and crown shapes remain estimates from land-cover tags, rather than a surveyed vegetation inventory.

Verification covers the real YBSU parking selector, two visible planes on the mapped apron, >1.5 km separation from the old airport centre, scenery rebuilds, leaving/returning, live map labels and actual on-foot boarding. ATC checks cover the button and T, review/approval, valid taxi/flight permission, runway conflicts, storm refusal, expiry, departure completion and airborne landing selection. A real mapped wood area produces over 1,200 forest trees with hundreds of undergrowth clumps, while a 20% vegetation setting reduces canopy substantially; sampled trunks avoid roads, water and airfield surfaces. Existing takeoff, fixed/retractable gear, aircraft damage, runway geometry and unarmed-civilian regressions remain tested. These counts describe the test area, not an FPS guarantee.
