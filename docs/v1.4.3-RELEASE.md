# v1.4.3 — Street Sessions

Free roam gains three short, repeatable road challenges through **ACTIVITIES**:

| Session | Goal | Time | Base reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Overtake Rush | Five close passes of moving civilian traffic without contact | 90 seconds | $1,800 |
| Drift Session | 800 points in a drift-capable road car | 120 seconds | $2,200 |
| Clean Getaway | Begin a two-star pursuit, be witnessed and escape | 180 seconds | $4,000 |

Near misses also build cash combos during ordinary driving. Multipliers rise through eight passes; pending cash banks after four seconds without another pass. Collision, teleport, admin test mode or disabled collisions clears pending rewards. Overtake/drift runs end on a crash or leaving the vehicle. Getaway allows an on-foot escape or hijacking another vehicle. Death, garage entry and invalid movement interrupt it. Completed sessions have a short cooldown; existing jobs cannot run alongside them. Rewards feed the saved career and existing competition categories, using a connected server's reward multiplier. Public career scores remain client reported.

The visual pass adds warmer low-angle daylight, cool ambient shadows, visible sun/moon discs, asphalt aggregate and subtle surface cracks, weather-dependent wet-road sheen, shared paint/window reflections and smoother foliage. The previous artificial emissive paint glow is removed with cinematic lighting enabled. Imported Life Models retain their source materials and existing reflection treatment. Balanced resolution uses normal browser filtering; High/Maximum use filtered shadows. **H → Graphics → Cinematic lighting / surface detail** toggles the treatment and saves the choice.

Foliage blobs now share 12 vertices across their existing 20 triangles instead of duplicating 60 face vertices. This reduces foliage vertex data by 80% and allows smooth normals, with small per-plant tonal variation. Shared textures and environment maps avoid per-car capture passes. This does not promise a fixed FPS gain or GTA V fidelity; scenery remains based on the existing Sunshine Coast map, inferred architecture and available assets.

The admin panel now starts with **Select a player**. Its choices are player names, yourself and all connected players. The selected name and current status appear underneath; the vehicle is context. Duplicate names get a short identity suffix. Selection follows the same player ID through vehicle changes, walking and lobby/garage presence. Temporary scene-node replacement cannot silently redirect commands to yourself. Disconnected targets remain visibly unavailable, their action buttons disable, and stale submissions return an error.

Money/wanted and freeze controls target the selected person. Vehicle effects apply to that person's current model. The catalogue is collapsed under **Give this player a new vehicle**, used only to grant a model. Action results identify the recipient. Existing local code, hosted-server owner authentication and signed public owner grants remain required; no credentials are embedded or published.

Verification covers real moving-traffic pass detection and cash banking, drift and witnessed-pursuit payouts, crash/teleport/timeout exclusions, graphics toggle persistence, wet/dry materials, filtered shadows, foliage buffer dimensions and browser rendering. Two-browser relay tests cover player selection through model changes, on-foot actions, money/grants, repair, lobby presence, disconnects and authenticated moderation regressions. The tutorial explains the new activities and crash risks.
