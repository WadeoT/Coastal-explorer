# v1.4.4 — Vehicle Model Repair

The nine supplied Life Models now use separate surface rules for paint, windows, lamp lenses, mirrors, interiors, rubber and metal. Lamp lenses retain colour and more surface opacity instead of disappearing like windscreens. Mirrors are opaque and reflective. Rubber/interiors have subdued highlights, and untextured interiors use darker cabin tones. Imported materials no longer receive the procedural car-paint texture intended for generated models; authored textures and liveries remain.

The BMW G81's main material was named `m3g81law`, which did not match the paint-name checks. It is now a paint surface, so garage repainting changes the main body. Existing custom colours and the authored paint textures on other models continue to work.

Asset cleanup removes 1,495 repeated same-facing faces from the BMW and 458 duplicate/degenerate faces from the Hornet, plus a few invalid faces in other affected assets. Opposite-facing lamp shells are retained. Cleaned assets keep valid positions, normals, UVs, colours and indices.

The ADAC rescue helicopter's near model increases from roughly 32,000 to 56,000 triangles. Blade classification follows the source's tilted rotor plane, putting previously fixed blade faces into the rotating assembly. Angle-aware normal smoothing preserves 40-degree creases and welds identical vertex attributes, reducing the high mesh from 167,969 to 44,888 vertices at the same triangle count. The distant/remote mesh stays around 8,000 triangles. The source has one plain material and no image textures, so yellow rescue paint, dark glazing, skid/metal and rotor regions are inferred from geometry. This improves separation and visibility but is not an exact authored ADAC livery or a newly surveyed aircraft model. Other imported silhouettes and real model identities are retained.

Verification covers all nine vehicles loading and simulating, finite GPU buffers, original car interiors, wheel suspension, visible crash deformation and repair, lower-detail instances, the rescue rotor and fighter landing gear. Additional checks cover surface classification, complete rotor extent, bounded distant detail, BMW repainting and repeated showroom changes. All nine shop pictures are regenerated from the repaired in-game geometry. Original creator credits and individual model licences remain in the game and [model credits](../docs/LIFE-MODELS.md).
